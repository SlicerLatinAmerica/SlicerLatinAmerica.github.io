3D Slicer: Ultrasound Images

Academic event focused on the use of 3D Slicer for Ultrasound Images.

June 6, 2025 from 12 to 4 PM

Location: Universidad del Valle de México (UVM), Toluca, Mexico
🌐 Event Website 📸 Event Photo Gallery

Description

The Slicer Toluca Workshop focused on Ultrasound Images. It was held on June 6, 2025 at Universidad del Valle de México (UVM).

Agenda

  • 12:00-1:00 PM Keynote Lecture: The role of the biomedical engineer in medical imaging: success stories in clinical and research applications using the 3D Slicer (Dr. Sonia Pujol)
  • 1:00-2:00 PM Lecture: Basic concepts and physics of ultrasound (LBM. Enrique Hernandez Laredo)
  • 2:00-2:30 PM Coffee break
  • 2:30-3:00 PM Workshop: Ultrasound image acquisition (Dr. Edgar Arturo Rodríguez Ramírez)
  • 3:00-4:00 PM Workshop: Three-dimensional estimation and segmentation using 3D Slicer and ultrasound images (Ing. Leidy Mora)
