Description

Sonia Pujol participated in the CZI Open Science 2025 annual meeting, held October 19–22, 2025 in Carlsbad, California. The Chan Zuckerberg Initiative (CZI) Open Science program supports open-source software tools and infrastructure essential for biomedical research.

The 3D Slicer for Latin America project is supported by the CZI Essential Open Source Software for Science program. The meeting brought together grantees, open-source developers, and researchers to share progress and collaborate on advancing open science.
