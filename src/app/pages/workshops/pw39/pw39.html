39th NA-MIC Project Week (PW39)

3D Slicer for Latin America — Tutorial Maker & Localization

June 12–16, 2023

Location: École de Technologie Supérieure, Montreal, Canada
🔗 Slicer for LA Project Page 🌐 PW39 Website

Description

The 39th NA-MIC Project Week was a hybrid event held at École de Technologie Supérieure, Montreal, Canada, from June 12 to 16, 2023. The Latin American team participated online with the 3D Slicer for Latin America project, focusing on the development of the Tutorial Maker infrastructure and the localization of 3D Slicer into Spanish and Portuguese.

This edition marked one of the first dedicated project pages for the 3D Slicer for Latin America initiative, bringing together team members from Brazil, Mexico, and collaborators worldwide.
