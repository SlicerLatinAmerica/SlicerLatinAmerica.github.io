3D Slicer: AI-based Technology Workshop for Rehabilitation Medicine Physicians

Academic event focused on the role of the rehabilitation medicine physician in the field of medical imaging, with a special focus on the use of 3D Slicer software and AI-based technologies.

October 8 & 10, 2025
9:00 - 11:00 HR

Location: Facultad de Medicina, Universidad Autónoma del Estado de México
