3D Slicer Ribeirão Preto Workshop: AI-driven technology for Radiologists

Join us for an interactive workshop exploring AI-powered image segmentation using the 3D Slicer platform. Designed for radiology residents, this session will introduce practical tools and techniques to enhance imaging workflows through image segmentation and artificial intelligence.

The event is free, but registration is mandatory and is on a first-come, first-served basis. Limited to 30 participants.
